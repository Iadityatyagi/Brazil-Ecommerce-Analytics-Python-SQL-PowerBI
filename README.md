# 🇧🇷 Brazilian E-Commerce Analytics | Olist
### End-to-End Data Analytics Project | Python • PostgreSQL • Excel • Power BI

![Project Banner](Power_BI/Screenshots/01 - Project Overview.png)

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an end-to-end analysis of the **Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset**, containing approximately 100,000 orders from 2016 to 2018.

The objective is to analyze the Brazilian e-commerce business from multiple perspectives, including sales performance, customer behavior, customer retention, product performance, seller contribution, payment methods, delivery efficiency, and customer satisfaction.

By combining Python, PostgreSQL, Excel, and Power BI, this project transforms raw e-commerce data into meaningful business insights that can support better business decisions.

**Project Type:** End-to-End Data Analytics  
**Domain:** E-Commerce / Retail Analytics  
**Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset  
**Period:** 2016–2018

---

## 🎯 Business Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are:

- Analyze overall revenue, order volume, and business performance.
- Understand customer purchasing behavior and retention.
- Identify high-value customers using RFM analysis.
- Evaluate product categories and seller performance.
- Analyze payment preferences and installment patterns.
- Investigate delivery performance and delays.
- Measure customer satisfaction through review ratings.
- Identify business opportunities using data-driven insights.

## 🔄 Project Workflow

The project follows a structured data analytics workflow:

1. **Data Collection:** Obtained the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset containing multiple related CSV tables.
2. **Data Cleaning:** Used Python to inspect, clean, and prepare the raw datasets.
3. **Database Management:** Imported structured data into PostgreSQL and established relationships between tables.
4. **SQL Analysis:** Performed joins, aggregations, filtering, and business-oriented queries.
5. **Excel Validation:** Used Excel to inspect and validate data and analytical results.
6. **Data Modeling:** Connected the prepared data to Power BI and developed the analytical model.
7. **DAX Development:** Created calculated measures and business KPIs.
8. **Dashboard Development:** Built a seven-page interactive dashboard to communicate business performance and insights.

## 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis provides several useful business observations:

1. **Customer Retention:** Customer acquisition and repeat purchasing analysis help identify opportunities to improve customer loyalty.
2. **Customer Value:** RFM segmentation helps distinguish high-value customers from less engaged customer groups.
3. **Product Performance:** Category and seller analysis helps identify major revenue contributors and best-selling products.
4. **Payment Behavior:** Payment method and installment analysis help understand customer payment preferences.
5. **Delivery Efficiency:** Delivery time and late delivery analysis help identify operational improvement opportunities.
6. **Customer Satisfaction:** Approximately 77.1% of reviews were positive, highlighting the overall positive review share while leaving room to investigate negative customer experiences.
